Your videos now actually play on every PC, with no Steam setting to hunt for.

## What's new

- **Steam plays your videos out of the box.** On a fresh Steam, the *Random startup movie* option is off, so Steam
  keeps playing its own video even when yours are installed. After you install or import a video (or a pack), the app
  now detects this and offers to turn the option on for you: Steam then picks one of your enabled videos at each start.
- If Steam is open, the app closes it cleanly, applies the change and starts it again. Quit your games first.
- Safe by design: only that single setting is changed in Steam's `config.vdf`, the rest of the file is kept byte for
  byte, and the previous file is saved next to it as `config.vdf.bvm-backup`. If the file looks unusual, the app
  leaves it alone and tells you how to do it by hand.
- A banner in the **Installed** tab shows when Steam ignores your videos, with a **Turn on** button.

## Fixes

- The "video installed" messages no longer tell you to pick the video in Steam's settings when Steam already shuffles
  your enabled videos.
