Like your favourite videos from the app, and a one-click install on Linux.

## What's new

- **Sign in with Steam.** A *Sign in with Steam* button at the top of the window opens Steam's real sign-in page
  (password or QR code from the Steam app): the app never sees your password and only keeps the steamdeckrepo.com
  session, encrypted for your Windows account. Once signed in, your avatar and name replace the button.
- **Like videos** with the heart on each card or in the preview panel; the like is sent to steamdeckrepo.com and the
  count updates with the site's answer.
- **My likes**: a new filter in the catalog shows only the videos you liked (on the site or in the app).
- **Linux: Flatpak package.** `BootVideoManager-1.3.0-x86_64.flatpak` installs with a double click from your software
  center (Linux Mint, Steam Deck desktop mode, GNOME, KDE…) and includes everything: video previews and Steam sign-in
  work without installing anything else.

## Fixes

- Linux: the sign-in window now works with WebKitGTK.
- The Flatpak copy does not look for GitHub updates: they come from your software center.
