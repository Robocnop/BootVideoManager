Share your selection of startup videos with your friends in one file.

## What's new

- **Share › Export my videos** (Installed tab): saves your catalog videos, with their enabled or disabled state, and
  the Steam stock intros you enabled into a small `.bvmpack` file.
- **Share › Import a pack**: before anything happens, the app shows what the pack will do (videos to download, already
  installed, no longer available). It then downloads the missing videos from steamdeckrepo.com through the usual
  queue, verified as always, and gives each video the state it had in the pack.
- **Keep only the pack's videos** (optional): disables your other videos so Steam only plays the pack's. Nothing is
  deleted; switch them back on at any time.
- A pack only holds references, never video files: it stays tiny and can safely be shared. Videos imported from your
  own `.webm` files and those of Steam's cache are not included (the export tells you how many were left out).
- Handy as a **backup** too: export your selection, and import it again after reinstalling Steam or on another PC.
